Capabilities

Safety you can check, not just hope for.

Guidance and control

Flight control and mission guidance for unmanned aircraft systems, from vehicle dynamics to onboard autonomy.

Modeling and simulation

High-fidelity models of flight vehicles and their environments, built on open, composable scientific computing tools.

Formal safety guarantees

Mathematical guarantees on where an aircraft can and cannot go, under stated model and disturbance bounds, so safety is established before flight testing begins.
